## Tier 2 — premium London

- **HiddenCity**: £19/player, 3–4hr SMS games; 1,822 TripAdvisor reviews
  since ~2012 → low-hundreds-of-thousands lifetime players (inference, LOW).
- **A Door in a Wall**: since 2008; public tickets ~£30pp (dated); bespoke
  corporate is the real business.
- **Agent November**: £119 per team of 3–7 (≈£17–40pp), since 2014, 4.8★.

## Tier 3 — Treasure Trails UK (best category P&L proxy)

- £9.99/trail (≈£2.50/head), 1,200+ trails, **2M players since 2005**;
  ~7.9K Trustpilot reviews at 4.6. Small-company accounts (turnover not
  disclosed); read-through: **£0.5–1.5M/yr, ~20 years profitable —
  lifestyle-scale, not venture-scale** (inference, LOW).

## Tier 6 — UK corporate

- **Team Tactics**: GPS hunts **from £65pp +VAT** (30 pax ≈ £1,950+/event).
- **ClueGo**: **£55–65pp fully managed** (iPads, host, leaderboard), groups
  6–600. Fire Hazard Citydash public tickets £15.
- London team-building averages £90–120pp; UK away-day spend ~£200M/yr
  (LOW); global team-building $12.4B, 9.2% CAGR.

## Escape rooms (adjacency benchmark)

- US ~2,000 facilities (Dec 2025), **flat since 2023 — matured**; avg ticket
  ~$32.50; **avg revenue per venue $250–350K/yr**; room build $20–50K.
- UK avg £18.67pp (range £15–35); UK market $409M (2022) → $1.29B (2032 proj).

## Structural takeaways

1. Consumer self-guided pricing clusters at **€7–15/person or €22–27/team**
   — a low-ARPU volume game. (Fedora at £40–50/team ≈ £11–14/head sits
   between Questo and CityDays, defensible for a premium racing product.)
2. **Nobody has made venture-scale money on consumer self-guided hunts**:
   the only VC-backed pure-play raised $2.1M in 7 years; its funded peer
   exited small; the survivors are bootstrapped lifestyle businesses
   (Let's Roam ~$5M est., Treasure Trails ~£1M/yr for 20 years).
3. The **10× price jump to corporate (£55–65pp) and premium hosted
   (£19–40pp)** is where per-unit economics improve.
4. Marketplace platform take is ~25% (creators keep up to 75%).
