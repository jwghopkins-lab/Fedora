## 2. Category and neighbour markets

- Self-guided tour app market: **$1.2–2.2B (2024)** → $3.7B by 2033 (★).
- Escape rooms global **~$10B**, 12–15% CAGR (★★). UK: $409M, 531 venues /
  1,594 rooms (★★★ census). US: 56% of adults have ever played (★★).
- Tours & activities overall: **$156–215B (2024)** (★★). GetYourGuide €1B
  revenue / €4B+ GMV / 33M bookings, profitable (★★★). Viator ~$4.2B gross
  bookings (★★★). Leaders combined <10% of market — fragmented; only ~33%
  of experience bookings happen online yet (Arival ★★★).
- Direct comps: **Questo 1M+ lifetime players** (3,000 quests, 1,000+
  cities, raised only $1.5M); **CluedUpp 5M+ lifetime players** (★★ company
  claims). No analyst names "city exploration games" as a category.
- Corporate team building: global **~$12B (2024)**, ~9% CAGR (★★).
  UK £100–200/employee/yr typical; London finance/tech £500–1,000/head;
  50-staff event £3–6K; derived ~55K paid team events/yr in London (★).

## 3. Participation benchmarks

- Sandemans free tours: **>2M guests/yr across ~20 cities ≈ 0.5–1% of a
  city's overnight visitors** — best attach-rate proxy (★★).
- Escape rooms: ~3–3.5% of UK/US population per year (derived ★).
- Geocaching: 1.85M people found ≥1 cache in 2024 (★★★).
- Experience buyers stack >4 activities per trip (Arival ★★★).
- Puzzle culture: 46% of UK adults attempted a crossword last year (~25M);
  hard-core hunt community ~10K-scale globally (MIT MH ~3–5K solvers,
  Puzzled Pint >3K/month, DASH low-1000s); ~350K people searched for the
  Fenn treasure; Masquerade sold ~1M copies.

## 4. Serviceable market (players/year, 10 cities)

Effective capture rate = awareness × participation-given-aware:

| Parameter | Low | Med | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tourist effective rate | 0.10% | 0.40% | 1.20% |
| Local effective rate | 0.06% | 0.32% | 0.90% |

High tourist rate ≈ Sandemans capture; high local rate ≈ ¼ of mature
escape-room penetration.

| Scenario | Players/yr (10 cities) | Gross at €12 ticket |
|---|---|---|
| Low | **~265K** | €3.2M |
| Medium | **~1.12M** | €13.5M |
| High (5–7-yr ceiling, not yr 1) | **~3.3M** | €40M |

Top-5 cities by opportunity: Paris, New York, London, Rome, Singapore.
Corporate layer: 150 events/city/yr × 25 heads × £50 ≈ **£1.9M** extra at
better margins.

**Read:** Low ≈ what one competently executed app captures (consistent with
Questo's lifetime numbers). Medium ≈ total category demand with real
marketing. High ≈ category matured to escape-room-adjacent penetration.

**Sensitivities:** (1) tourist awareness — OTA distribution is the single
highest-leverage lever; (2) day-trippers excluded = material upside;
(3) Paris/Rome/Barcelona assume localization — English-only cuts their
tourist base 30–40%.
