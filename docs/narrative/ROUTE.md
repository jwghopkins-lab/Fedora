# GROUND TRUTH — route surgery

Phase 5. The existing 22 parts, cut and rebuilt against the locked decisions
(D1–D5, D7, D8). **Shape only** — no clue text is written here. Text is Phase 6.

The count comes out at **22 again**, which is not a coincidence I engineered so
much as one I checked for and was pleased to find: 17 parts survive untouched
or retargeted, 4 are cut, 1 is replaced in place, 4 are new.

---

## The single best find in this phase

**Cutting Cavell frees the hinge, and St Martin-in-the-Fields is standing in it.**

Part 16 (Cavell, St Martin's Place) sits exactly where Act 2 becomes Act 3 —
the pivot from the square into St Martin's Lane. It has to be replaced by
*something*, because the geography needs a beat there.

It should be **the church itself**. It is the clerk's parish. It is the vestry
whose local standards were dispersed in 1900 when the vestries were abolished.
The Victorian hand of the diary wrote his survey book in that building. It is
on the pavement, it is unmissable, it is free, and it does narrative work Cavell
never could — the players walk past the address on the diary's flyleaf without
being told, and find out later.

## The new running order

### Act 1 — Westminster and Whitehall *(5 parts, was 3)*

| # | Part | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The fire** — where fire burned the length of England | **keep verbatim.** The best opening in the script; do not touch it |
| 2 | **The Fawcett plinth** | **retarget** to **Ray Strachey**. Same stone, same mechanic, no rebuild — but the first name they read is now two hops from the 1884 meridian table |
| 3 | **The Cenotaph** — Whitehall | **NEW.** Lutyens. No straight lines: verticals meeting 1,000 ft up, horizontals arcing round a centre 900 ft below, 33 pages of arithmetic to make a stone look honest |
| 4 | **Mountbatten Green** — Horse Guards Road | **NEW.** Belsky's first appearance. A jam jar of coins in the left leg, unveiled 2 Nov 1983, cast by the **Meridian** Bronze Foundry, twelve days after the metre became a property of light |
| 5 | **Recall our measures** → Trafalgar | **keep** (was 3) |

**Why this is the biggest single improvement available.** Right now the walk
from Parliament Square to Trafalgar is about ten minutes of nothing — one "dig"
clue and a hike up Whitehall. Parts 3 and 4 do not *add* ten minutes; they
convert ten dead minutes into the two beats that set up the entire second half.
Both are on the pavement, both are true, and both belong to characters who come
back. Route: Parliament Square → Whitehall for the Cenotaph → west through King
Charles Street to Horse Guards Road → Mountbatten → north to the Mall and
Admiralty Arch → Trafalgar. No backtracking.

**Part 15 was already doing our work and nobody noticed.** Its existing
explainer ends: *"this square holds two obsolete measures. One set in brass in
public so it could never be lost, and one sealed inside a man's head where
nobody can ever see it."* That is the promise/proof structure of D7, already
written, six months early. It only ever needed the terrace measurement to
answer it.

**Part 16 is the turn.** A published national dimension — 185 ft — was wrong by
fourteen and a half feet for about 150 years, until a laser survey in 2006 found
169 ft 3 in. Entirely factual, entirely checkable, standing in the middle of
everything. It is the moment a player stops treating the walk as trivia. It has
to come *after* Belsky, so the question "what else was never re-measured?"
arrives with somewhere to go.

## Where the cord goes *(D7)*

- **Issued at the start**, in the pack with the diary. Not found at the end.
- **Used at part 11**, on the terrace brass, because that is the only genuine
  public standard on the route and they are already standing on it.
- **Means something at part 22**, in Covent Garden, when the number they wrote
  down two hours earlier turns out to be the answer to Belsky.

So the measuring act sits mid-walk and the meaning lands at the end. The route
does **not** loop back to Trafalgar to fix this — that was the lazy option and
it is rejected.

## Problems to resolve before Phase 6

1. **The lions: dog or cat?** The script says "Dog-like paws" and "he worked
   the rest from his own dog." Published accounts say the paws are a **domestic
   cat's** and the backs concave where a lion's is convex, with the cause being
   the late Turin cast plus a zoo carcass that decomposed too fast. The "his own
   dog" story needs a source before it goes near a clue — this is precisely the
   detail a player checks on the pavement.
2. **Hints for the two unlimited parts.** Old 4 and 17 (new **6** and **18**)
   still have fewer than two hints, so they cannot carry lives.
3. **Gates.** Currently on old 2, 4, 18 → now **2, 6, 19**. The four new parts
   may want their own; and the testing skip still has to come out once the radii
   are walked.
4. **Database.** This replaces all 22 clue rows — another content migration in
   the shape of v9. No SQL until the text exists.
5. **The diary's entry point is still unplaced.** The frame is locked (D1: in
   character from the moment it opens) but nothing in the route yet *opens* it.
   Phase 6.
