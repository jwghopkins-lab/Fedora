## Before anything else — the finding that reframes the project

I went looking for the movement's history and found that **the frame we have
been working in is wrong**, and the true version is far better.

We have been writing *crank versus establishment*: Smyth does pyramid metrology,
the Royal Society destroys him for it, and the truth he blundered into goes down
with him.

**That is not what happened. Both sides were doing pyramid metrology.**

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **1864–65** | The **Ordnance Survey surveys Jerusalem** — six Royal Engineers under Captain Charles Wilson, directed by **Colonel Sir Henry James**, engraved **at the Ordnance Survey Office, Southampton** | T1 |
| **1866** | **A. R. Clarke publishes the *Comparisons of the Standards of Length* — from that same office, under that same director** | T1 |
| **1868–69** | The **Ordnance Survey surveys the Peninsula of Sinai**, Wilson and Palmer, again under James | T1 |
| **1869** | **Sir Henry James publishes *Notes on the Great Pyramid of Egypt and the Cubits used in its Design*, Southampton** | **T1** |

**The Director General of the Ordnance Survey — Clarke's own boss — personally
published on the cubits of the Great Pyramid, from the building where the
world's length standards were being compared.** The state's surveying arm mapped
Jerusalem and Sinai in the same decade. And five years later the Royal Society
rejected Smyth's pyramid paper.

And it was not a quiet disagreement. In **1869** Smyth delivered a lecture to the
Edinburgh Photographic Society and published it under this title:

> ***A Poor Man's Photography at the Great Pyramid in the Year 1865: Compared
> with that of the Ordnance Survey Establishment Subsidized by London Wealth,
> and under the Orders of Henry James.***

**That is a public attack on the Director General of the Ordnance Survey, by
name, in a title.** Smyth's antagonist was never only Airy. It was Southampton.

**What this does to the story.** Smyth was not an outsider shouting at a closed
door. He was **on the losing side of a turf war inside the same enterprise**,
and the enterprise was the one that computed the shape of the Earth from a case
of brass bars. The 1874 rejection stops being contempt for an eccentric and
becomes *the winning faction closing the subject*.

Clarke sat in that building for twenty-seven years, under the man who wrote the
cubits pamphlet, comparing the standards. **He resigned in 1881 and no source
says why.**

I am not going to pretend that gap and this context are unrelated. But note what
the story now needs from us: **nothing.** The invention was the conspiracy of
silence. The record supplies a documented professional war over exactly the
question, with our man in the middle of it.

**Three rules follow, and they are not negotiable.**

   the debrief, and never used for colour.
2. **Nobody in our story is a sympathetic British Israelite.** Smyth is

   wanted the cubits because he was a surveyor. Neither needed a bloodline.
   **That separability is the point of the whole book**: a true observation and
   a poisonous explanation travelling in the same parcel, and everybody throwing
   out the parcel.

## The people

**Richard Brothers** (1757–1824) · The founder. Naval officer, millenarian,
self-declared **Prince of the Hebrews** and "Nephew of the Almighty". **Arrested
in 1795 for treasonably prophesying the death of the King** and confined as a
criminal lunatic. Defended in the Commons by an MP. — *The doctrine that makes
the monarch a descendant of David begins with a man imprisoned for prophesying
against the monarch.*

**John Wilson** (1799–1870) · The systematiser. *Our Israelitish Origins*, 1840.
Made it a lecture circuit and a movement.

**Edward Hine** (1825–1891) · The populariser. Claimed enormous sales, toured
America, founded the British-Israel Identity Corporation in 1880. **Fell out with
Edward Wheeler Bird**, and the two ran rival operations.

**John Taylor** (1781–1864) · Publisher and amateur. Wrote the pyramid inch into
existence in 1859 **from Newton's postulated cubit**, without ever visiting
Egypt. Everything metrological in the movement descends from a man working at a
desk from other people's figures.

**Charles Piazzi Smyth** (1819–1900) · Astronomer Royal for Scotland. Genuinely
distinguished — the Tenerife expedition proved that altitude improves seeing, and
he was right. Then Giza, the pyramid inch, the anti-metric campaign, the
Institute vice-presidency, the attack on Sir Henry James, the campaign for a
pyramid prime meridian, the 1874 rejection, the 1888 resignation. Died childless
at Ripon.

**Sir Henry James** (1803–1877) · **Director General of the Ordnance Survey
1854–75.** Sent Royal Engineers to Jerusalem and Sinai. Published *Notes on the
Great Pyramid of Egypt and the Cubits used in its Design*, Southampton, 1869.
**Clarke's director for the whole of the period that matters.** — *The
establishment's own pyramid metrologist, and the reason "crank versus
establishment" is the wrong frame.*

**Charles Latimer** (1827–1888) · American **railway engineer**, president of the
International Institute. *The Battle of the Standards*, 1880.

**Flinders Petrie** (1853–1942) · Went to Giza because **his father believed**.
Measured properly, disproved it, and became the father of scientific archaeology.

**David Davidson** (1884–1956) · **Structural engineer.** Set out to disprove
Smyth, converted, published in 1924, predicted the end for **16 September 1936**,
and was still lecturing at Westminster in October 1937.

**Princess Alice, Countess of Athlone** (1883–1981) · Granddaughter of Queen
Victoria. **Patron-in-chief of the British-Israel-World Federation until her
death.** — *A real person, recently alive, who did nothing wrong and must not be
made to. Her patronage is the fact; anything more is off limits.*

**Charles Warren** (1840–1927) · Royal Engineer. Excavated the Temple Mount
1867–70 for the PEF. Later Commissioner of Police during the Whitechapel murders.

## Where a fiction fits — seven points, ranked

### 1 · The twenty-four years between Wilson and Taylor · **T5, and the best structural gap**
Wilson's doctrine in **1840** has no metrology in it. Taylor's *pyramid inch*
arrives in **1859**. Nothing documents how the bloodline claim and the
measurement claim were welded together, or by whom.

**That weld is the whole premise of our book, and the record does not say who
made it.** A fiction placed here contradicts nothing, because there is nothing
to contradict.

### 2 · What the Federation did in 1950–51 · **T5, and the best scene**
The organisation's central relic is stolen, broken, and repaired by a mason who
had made two copies of it and had planned to substitute one. **A royal patron-in-
chief was in post.** And I can find no record of the Federation's response.

An organisation with a royal patron, whose entire physical claim rests on one
stone, saying nothing on the record while that stone is out of its case for four
months. **Fiction can occupy that silence completely.**

### 3 · The Hine–Bird schism · **T5**
Two men ran the movement and fell out. What the quarrel was actually about is
thinly recorded. **A schism is a place to put a reason.**

### 4 · 1895–97, the near-miss · **T4/T5**
The Select Committee recommended compulsion; the Act declined to compel.
**Whether the anti-metric lobby caused that is not established anywhere**, and
the lobby's leading British figure was a serving Astronomer Royal. Cause is
unprovable in both directions — which is precisely the safe ground.

### 5 · The Committee on Standard Time · **T5**
It existed and published. **What it argued is not summarised in anything I could
reach.** A documented committee with an undocumented position, on the exact
subject of our SKY thread, in the exact years of the meridian conference.

### 6 · Clarke's resignation, now with a context · **T1 event, T5 reason**
Twenty-seven years at Southampton, under the man who published on the cubits,
comparing the standards — then out in 1881, three years before the fortnight, and
no source says why. **We do not need to state a reason. The chapter only has to
put the facts in this order.**

### 7 · The membership numbers · **T4**
Five thousand by a historian's count against the Federation's own claims of
hundreds of thousands. **A movement that could not honestly say how big it was.**

## What this does to the spine

**Three changes, and one is structural.**

**1 · The antagonist changes shape.** Airy stays, but **Sir Henry James is the
better figure** — because he is not an opponent of the idea, he is a *rival
practitioner* of it, and he outranked everyone in the room that mattered.
"The establishment suppressed the pyramid" is a thriller cliché. **"The
establishment was doing it too, and better funded, and Smyth called them out by
name in a lecture title" is a fact.**

**2 · The BAR and SKY threads become one.** Smyth fought the yard and the
meridian in the same campaign, for the same reason. Airy fixed both. **There is
one conflict in this story, not two.**

**3 · Petrie and Davidson are the book's argument about evidence, and they are
free.** Two men checked the same claim. **Petrie checked and was freed of it.
Davidson checked and was captured by it.** Same act, opposite results. That
belongs at the front of the modern thread, because it is what the narrator is
about to do.
